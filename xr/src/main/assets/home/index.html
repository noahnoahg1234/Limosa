Controls in VR

1 finger on empty space: look around. 2 fingers: the left finger moves the left controller, the right finger the right one. Tap Hand: Move to switch fingers to Depth (push hands forward and back) or Aim (rotate the controllers).
LT / RT are the triggers (select), LG / RG the grips (squeeze), X Y A B the face buttons. The circles at the bottom are thumbsticks; a quick tap on a stick clicks it. Gyro lets you look around by moving the phone, View: 3D switches to side-by-side stereo for a phone headset, ⟲ Center resets the view and hands, the back button leaves VR.

Settings

Apps built for the browser (WebXR) run here. Native headset apps (APK files from a store or SideQuest) cannot: they need the headset's own operating system and hardware.
